
# Thesis Evaluation Rubric (ISIST)

The final grade is divided into two main components: **Director Evaluation (60%)** and **Juror/Defense Evaluation (40%)**.

## 1. Director's Evaluation (60% Total)
The director evaluates the student's performance throughout the development of the project.

* **Process (Proceso) - 10%:** Evaluation of the student's work habit, consistency, and evolution during the thesis.
* **Design (Diseño) - 15%:** Quality and rigor of the engineering design and architectural decisions.
* **Solution (Solución) - 15%:** The effectiveness of the final solution in addressing the proposed problem.
* **Quality Control (Control de Calidad) - 10%:** Implementation of testing and validation protocols.
* **Memory (Memoria) - 10%:** The director's assessment of the written document and technical report.

## 2. Jurors' Evaluation: Memory & Product (36% Total)
Two jurors evaluate the deliverables and the final prototype.

* **Written Expression (4%):** Ability to express the work clearly, effectively, and in an organized manner within the document.
* **Environment & Tools Analysis (5%):** References and description of state-of-the-art work/applications. Analysis of the environment's strengths/weaknesses compared to the proposed solution.
* **Design (5%):** Designing engineering solutions subject to environmental constraints and integrating identified requirements.
* **Functional Prototype (8%):** Analysis and implementation of the developed prototype (functionalities, screens, etc.).
* **Quality Control (7%):** Description of the testing protocol and the definition of test scenarios.
* **Analysis & Conclusions (7%):** Impact analysis (social, technological, economic, and disciplinary), fulfillment of objectives, and results analysis.

## 3. Sustentation / Defense (4% Total)
This evaluates the oral presentation and defense of the project.

* **Presentation (Director) - 2%:** Evaluation by the director of the defense's clarity and structure.
* **Presentation (Jurors) - 2%:** Evaluation by the jurors of the defense's organization, pedagogical sense, and product demonstration.

---

## Honors (Mención de Honor)
A project is eligible for "Mención de Honor" if:
1.  The final grade is **equal to or greater than 4.5**.
2.  The decision is **unanimous** among the director and both jurors.
